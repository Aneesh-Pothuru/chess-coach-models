% claim: results-summary
\begin{abstract}
Clearing the engine before each second look lowered every device gap's point
estimate on the \Cases{} frozen games. The phone met the registered narrowing
rule; the desktop did not. Neither device established absolute agreement
with the routine. Every quality guard was inconclusive against the
\QualityMargin-point margin. The decision is \emph{do not keep: inconclusive}.
The current app stays in place. Lower disagreement with the routine did not
establish that a cleared look preserved its judgment against the reference.
\end{abstract}

\section{The question and the data}
% claim: candidate
The candidate clears the analyst's memory immediately before each initial
second look and its optional adaptive retry, at every profile. It exposes
the engine's existing \texttt{newGame} method on the review port, calls it,
and waits for readiness after \texttt{ucinewgame}. The initial look keeps
\VerifyNodes{} nodes and \Candidates{} candidates; an adaptive retry keeps
\AdaptiveNodes{} nodes. There is no reset between the candidate search and
the restricted search of the played move. Sweeps, flags, quotas, adaptation,
evidence retention, classification, tablebase, teaching and cache policy are
unchanged. Teaching searches and manual rechecks are outside the candidate.
The question is whether this change brings the devices closer to the routine
without worsening the second look's judgment.

% claim: data
The data are the predecessor's frozen games, in the same order, with both
players' moves: \Cases{} games and \Plies{} plies. \TimeControls{}
No game was filtered by time control. These are the player's games already
studied by \texttt{phone-vs-routine}, not a new holdout.
The measured app is \texttt{\AppCommit}; its records are at
\texttt{\RecordsCommit}. The predecessor's older published result is context;
the baseline for this test was rebuilt here.

% claim: instruments
The unchanged evidence builder made a canonical document for every arm,
profile and game. Each game used a fresh Node engine, \Engine,
\Threads{} thread, \HashSize{} MB hash and no evaluation cache.
Separate observed replays captured engine calls and verification evidence
before routine teaching could replace it. An accepted replay had to reproduce
the canonical positions, records, coverage and provenance exactly.

% claim: determinism
Before comparison, cleared raw evidence matched in all
\DeterminismComparisons{} cross-profile comparisons on
\DeterminismPositions{} positions at both verification budgets.
That small check did not require equal final reviews: profiles can select
different retries, retain different evidence or add routine teaching.

\section{The method and the rule}
% claim: gap-method
The app's \texttt{isSerious} reads the final records. A miss is a routine
serious ply absent from the device's serious set; an extra is a device serious
ply absent from the routine's set. Miss share divides by routine serious
plies; extra share divides by device serious plies. Each arm uses its own
routine. All played plies count, including both sides, terminal and tablebase
records. Unknown classes are not serious under that instrument. Estimates
pool counts over games. Changes are candidate minus baseline.

% claim: bootstrap
The paired bootstrap uses \Draws{} Poisson(\PoissonMean) draws over games,
seed \Seed, with the same game weights across arms, profiles and measures.
Ratios are subtracted within each draw. Ordinary percentile intervals have
\Confidence\% nominal coverage. The \Effects{} decision effects---the device
gap changes and profile quality changes---use Bonferroni intervals with
\CorrectedConfidence\% nominal coverage. Quantiles use linear interpolation.
Invalid estimates cannot pass.

% claim: decision-rule
A device's gap narrows when both point changes are negative, at least one
corrected upper bound is below zero, and the other corrected lower bound is
at most zero. Both devices must pass. Absolute agreement
requires every device share's ordinary upper bound below \AgreementLine\%.
This descriptive bar provides no alternate route to keeping the candidate.

% claim: quality-method
The quality guard uses, separately for each profile, the plies both arms
attempted to verify, including incomplete looks. It reads evidence before
teaching. A fresh \ReferenceNodes-node search with \Candidates{} candidates,
full history and no evaluation cache supplies the reference. An incomplete
or non-exact reference gets \ReferenceRetries{} fresh retry at the same
budget; the first valid attempt is used. An unresolved reference blocks the
affected guard. The app's \texttt{judgeMove} and \texttt{isSerious} give the
raw serious-or-not verdicts. Incomplete arm evidence counts as incorrect
against a valid reference. ``Error'' here means disagreement with this
reference, not a proven chess error.

% claim: decision-rule
Candidate minus baseline error must have a corrected upper bound strictly
below \QualityMargin{} percentage point at every profile. A corrected lower
bound above that margin rejects the candidate as worse; otherwise that guard
is inconclusive. Keep for a port only when both device gaps narrow and all
quality guards pass. Time and depth have no adoption threshold.

\subsection{The quality guard}
The \ReferencePositions{} distinct reference positions all resolved on their
first attempt. No common look was guard-incomplete in either arm, and all
\Draws{} bootstrap draws were valid. Yet every quality interval spans the
\QualityMargin-point margin (Table~\ref{tab:quality}). The phone's error

\DesktopErrorChange{} points (\DesktopErrorInterval). Its corrected lower

more reference disagreement; it does not meet the rule's rejection condition
of worsening beyond the margin. Nor does it establish noninferiority.

\section{Acquisition history}
% claim: acquisition
The original candidate routine acquisition failed exact reproduction. An
approved amendment rebuilt that entire batch while preserving the baseline
and device outputs. The replacement then encountered a human-policy failure
whose changed rows followed routine teaching, and a later search spanning
documented sleep. Their first outputs were preserved. A rule requiring proof

recorded. A later approved rule allowed a prescribed retry on the documented
sleep and downstream-row evidence without claiming deadline proof.

before any deciding metric was computed or read. The final accepted sample
contains \AcceptedRetries{} prescribed retries; each passed the unchanged
canonical equality check. No candidate, estimator, bootstrap or threshold
was changed after a deciding result.

\section{Discussion}
% claim: results-summary
The lower gap estimates are useful evidence about this fixed change, but
they are insufficient under its rule. Narrowing was established only for
the phone, and none of the quality guards established noninferiority.
Agreement between reviews and agreement with the reference are different
requirements; improving the former did not establish the latter here.

% claim: app
Nothing. This study supplies no app port or saved-review migration. A kept
candidate would clear the analyst before selected second looks and adaptive
retries on phone, desktop and routine. At the measured pin, Game Review opens
a saved device review first and offers the routine's review for comparison
when available. Existing saved judgments remain until an explicit rebuild.

% claim: limits
The frozen games are reused, so the game bootstrap describes uncertainty
within this sample rather than a new-player validation. The reference is
higher-budget Stockfish evidence, not ground truth; its fresh search path
may favor the candidate. The guard covers common attempted looks, not all
plies or coaching text. Actual devices have a \DeviceHashSize{} MB hash and
an evaluation cache; this serial, uncached Node study uses \HashSize{} MB.
It does not settle cache hits or interleaved analyst work on a device.

\section{Conclusion}
% claim: results-summary
Do not keep: inconclusive. The registered combination of narrowing and
preserved quality was not established. The current second look remains.
