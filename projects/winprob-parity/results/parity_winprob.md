App commit `d8b7712` (the pinned commit), generated by `projects/winprob-parity/parity.py`. White's win%, in percentage points.

| Range | Points | Largest gap | At | Lab | App |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| mate in 1 to 30 | 60 | 2.4553 | #+1 | 97.5447 | 100.0000 |

Within ±1000 cp the lab and the app compute the same curve. Past it the lab holds every evaluation, mates included, at 97.54% (or its mirror) while the app goes on towards 100%. The lab's blunder and candidate labels (a loss over 20 and over 10 points) are computed on the clamped curve, so a move whose evaluation passes ±1000 cp can lose up to 2.46 points less in the lab than in the app (twice that if it crosses from one side to the other), enough to flip a label near either threshold. Which clamp a ported model should use is a decision for its port; this report only says where the two part.
